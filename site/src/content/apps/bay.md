# Bay (Media Review & Approval)


Media review that puts the notes on the media: frame-accurate video annotations, real 3D model review, and per-reviewer decisions that actually mean something.

- Drop frame, timecode, and region annotations right on video and stills, so feedback lands on the exact moment instead of "around the 30 second mark, I think"
- Review 3D for real: upload an FBX, OBJ, or GLB, watch Bay convert it to GLB, and orbit/pan/zoom it in a three.js viewer with live triangle, material, and animation-clip stats
- Pin viewpoint-anchored annotations on a model and fly the camera straight back to them, so "fix the shoulder weighting" arrives at the angle where the shoulder is actually broken
- Record explicit per-reviewer decisions per version (Approve, Reject, Request Changes) so a sign-off is a fact, not a vibe
- Send token-gated public guest links to clients with no account, gated by a single 48-hex path token and nothing else
- Let AI agents review through the same tools, identity, and audit trail as humans, via 14 MCP tools
