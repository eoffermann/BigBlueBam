# Bureau


Bureau is the virtual office your remote team walks into: live floors, real rooms, and a presence widget that follows you everywhere in the suite.

- See who is around at a glance on a spatial floor map, then click a room to drop in and start a voice huddle.
- Knock on a closed office, ring one person to your screen, or hunt a teammate and jump to wherever they are - every move respects Do Not Disturb and access.
- Bring everyone in your room to the same Board canvas, Brief doc, or Bond deal with one click, with each recipient access-checked first.
- AI agents are first-class occupants: through the Bureau MCP tools they locate people, knock, summon, book rooms, and manage floors under the same permissions as the UI.
