# Blank (Forms)


Build forms and surveys with a visual editor, publish them to a public link, and watch the responses roll in.

- Drag-and-drop form builder with over 20 field types and multi-page support
- Public form pages that work for anonymous respondents, with org and project scoping when you need it
- Response analytics with totals, a 30-day submission trend, and per-field breakdowns, plus one-click CSV export
- AI agents can generate a form from a plain-language description, publish it, and summarize the responses for you
