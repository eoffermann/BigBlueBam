Review stills, video, audio, and 3D models in one place, with notes pinned exactly where they belong and one verdict per reviewer.

- Drop a file in and review it: a version stack, coordinate-anchored notes, and per-reviewer Approve / Changes / Reject decisions, all on the same page
- Notes are glued to the media, not the conversation: a video frame or in/out span, an image region box, or, on a 3D model, the exact spot on the mesh and the angle you were looking from
- Review FBX, OBJ, STL, GLB, and more: Bay converts the model to a browser-ready proxy, then lets you orbit, remember a viewpoint, and drop a surface spot that survives every orbit
- Hand an outside client a single token-gated link to view and comment, with optional expiry and a one-click revoke, no account required
- AI agents review through the very same tools and audit trail as people: an agent can open a review, post anchored QC findings, and propose a verdict
