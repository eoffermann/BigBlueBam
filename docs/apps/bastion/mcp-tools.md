# bastion MCP Tools


| Tool | Description | Parameters |
|------|-------------|------------|
| `bastion_acknowledge_drift` | Acknowledge that a control got worse (POST /drift/:id/acknowledge), with an optional note. It does not change the control or its task; it records who has seen it. 409 DRIFT_ALREADY_ACKNOWLEDGED. Needs bastion.drift.acknowledge. | `drift_id`, `note` |
| `bastion_adopt_framework` | Adopt a built-in framework (SOC 2 or ISO 27001) for the organization (POST /frameworks), optionally with an audit target date and period. Creates the framework's controls, reusing any control another adopted framework already has, and links them to its requirements. Next step: bastion_compile_framework to propose checks. Needs bastion.framework.write; 409 if the framework is already adopted. | none |
| `bastion_answer_questionnaire_item` | Review one drafted answer (PATCH /questionnaire-items/:id): accepted (take the draft), edited (give final_answer and/or final_yes_no), needs_owner (assigned_to: the member who should answer it) or pending (undo). Reviewing is not approving: approval is a person's act in Bastion. Needs bastion.questionnaire.write. | `item_id`, `review_status`, `final_answer`, `final_yes_no`, `assigned_to` |
| `bastion_archive_framework` | Archive a framework (POST /frameworks/:id/archive). DESTRUCTIVE: controls that serve only this framework are retired with it. Two-step confirm: call once WITHOUT confirm_token to stage a token, then again WITH the returned confirm_token (same framework_id) within 5 minutes to archive. Needs bastion.framework.write. | `framework_id`, `confirm_token` |
| `bastion_attestation_status` | Policy acknowledgements. Without policy_id: every linked policy (GET /policies) with its Beacon article, audience, how often it must be re-read, owner, whether it is still shared with everyone, Beacon's expiry, and "X of Y have read the current version". With policy_id: who has read the current version and when, who has not, and who cannot even open the article in Beacon ("N people can't open this policy", from can_access per person) (GET /policies/:id/attestations). Read-only; needs bastion.policy.read or bastion.attestation.read. | `policy_id`, `status` |
| `bastion_audit_log` | Bastion's audit log, newest first (GET /audit): every change made in Bastion with who did it (person or agent), whether they were impersonated, how they signed in, and the details. Filter by action (e.g. auditor_room.create, settings.update) or subject_type; page with cursor. Read-only; floored: needs bastion.audit.read. | `action`, `subject_type`, `cursor`, `limit` |
| `bastion_auditor_room_view` | Exactly what an auditor sees in one auditor room (GET /auditor-rooms/:id/preview), without logging a visit: the controls in the room with their state words (Met, Not met, Not met risk accepted, Can't prove yet, Not assessed), the restricted items the room shares, accepted risks, the Statement of Applicability for ISO, the signed seal history with public keys, and any restore or missing-file notices. Read-only; floored: needs bastion.auditor_room.read. | none |
| `bastion_check_evidence_scan_again` | For an upload still waiting on its virus scan, check Bin again right now instead of waiting for the next automatic retry (POST /manual-evidence/:id/check-again; the review queue's "Check again"). If the scan finished clean (or scanning is off) the file is copied into Bastion's evidence store; an infected or failed scan blocks it. check_result is copied, copy_started (a large file is still being saved), still_pending, blocked or skipped. 409 NOT_PENDING_SCAN when the item is not waiting. Needs bastion.evidence.upload. | none |
| `bastion_compile_framework` | Propose checks for a framework's controls (POST /frameworks/:id/compile, 202 with the run). With an AI provider the run comes back `queued` and the worker tailors each control from the closed probe menu (the AI never writes queries; anything invalid is dropped with a reason, and a control the AI cannot help with gets the library suggestions); poll with action="list_runs" until it is succeeded. Without a provider, or with use_ai=false, the library suggestions are proposed and the run is already finished. Or list its past runs with action="list_runs" (GET /frameworks/:id/compile-runs, newest first; pass next_cursor back as cursor while has_more is true). The compile only PROPOSES: every check lands as proposed and a person must confirm it in Bastion (including "This is the complete list" for any list of systems). Re-running proposes nothing a control already has. A run already going returns 409 COMPILE_RUNNING. | `framework_id`, `action`, `use_ai`, `cursor`, `limit` |
| `bastion_control_status` | One control: its stored status and reason, setup state, derived applicability, every framework requirement it satisfies, and its checks (assertions) with their review state and, for population checks, the listed systems and whether a person confirmed the list (GET /controls/:id). With checks_only, just the checks, newest first (GET /controls/:id/assertions). Read-only. | `control_id`, `checks_only` |
| `bastion_coverage_gaps` | What to fix first (GET /gaps): stored gaps ranked by risk (control weight x severity x how close the audit is), or sort "quick_wins" for risk per unit of effort. Each gap names its control, owner, kind in plain words (Not met, Evidence not uploaded yet, Something new is not on the list, ...), its fix template and cost, and any drafted fix. Also returns the org-level "checks are stale" row (never stored per control) and "Accepted risks" (active exceptions with owner and expiry). status defaults to "active" (open plus waiting on a decided fix). Read-only. | none |
| `bastion_coverage_summary` | Readiness in one framework view (or all adopted frameworks). mode "summary" (default, GET /summary): the four numbers kept separate (Proven by the suite, Proven by evidence you provided, Not met with its "risk accepted" sub-count, Can't prove yet) plus "Not set up yet" and the denominator sentence ("41 of 64 controls checked; 23 not set up yet"), days to the nearest audit, banners, the top open gaps and evidence expiring soon. mode "map" (GET /coverage): every control with where its proof comes from (suite checks, feeds, uploaded files, self-attested statements), its state in plain words and when uploaded proof expires. Not-applicable controls are listed separately. Read-only; both the caller and the asker need bastion.control.read. | `asker_user_id`, `mode`, `framework_id` |
| `bastion_create_export` | Start building an audit packet (POST /exports, 202): a zip of the framework's controls and state words, the evidence and uploaded files in the period (at most 400 days), attestations, exceptions, drift, the Statement of Applicability for ISO, a summary PDF and a manifest with every signed seal and public key. include_restricted needs bastion.evidence.read_restricted (403 RESTRICTED_NOT_ALLOWED). An ISO packet is refused until every Annex A requirement has an applicability decision (409 SOA_INCOMPLETE). Follow progress with bastion_list_exports; a person downloads the zip in Bastion. Needs bastion.export.create. | `framework_id`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `include_restricted` |
| `bastion_create_feed` | Set up a machine feed (POST /feeds): an API-push feed that an outside system sends records to with its own per-feed key, or a Bolt-event feed (source_kind "bolt_event" with bolt_source and bolt_event_type) that records matching suite events. Records are frozen as evidence. Minting the push key is a credential act a person does in Bastion (no tool). Needs bastion.feed.manage; 409 FEED_KEY_TAKEN if the key is in use. | none |
| `bastion_create_policy` | Link a Beacon article as a policy people must read and acknowledge (POST /policies). Name the article by beacon_entry_id, or by beacon_slug (the last part of its Beacon link, /beacon/<slug>). The article must be shared with everyone (Beacon visibility Organization or Public; else 409 POLICY_NOT_SHARED). audience is everyone (default) or a list of members; cadence_days is how often it must be re-read (default 365). With control_id, Bastion proposes that control's two policy checks (read and acknowledged; kept current) pinned to this policy, for a person to confirm. Without control_id, Bastion does the same for the one control whose policy checks are not yet pinned to an active policy (if there is exactly one). 409 POLICY_ALREADY_LINKED. Needs bastion.policy.write (owners and admins). | none |
| `bastion_create_questionnaire` | Add a security questionnaire (POST /questionnaires) from a Bin file (xlsx or csv; give bin_asset_id, the file must already be in Bin and virus-scanned) or from pasted rows (pasted_text; tab-separated as copied from a spreadsheet, or csv). Bastion guesses which column holds the questions; check it with bastion_get_questionnaire and fix it with bastion_update_questionnaire before drafting. Optionally link a Bond deal. 409 FILE_NOT_SCANNED_YET, 400 QUESTIONNAIRE_TOO_LARGE (over the per-questionnaire row limit). Needs bastion.questionnaire.write. | `title`, `bin_asset_id`, `pasted_text`, `pasted_format`, `requester_label`, `bond_deal_id`, `due_date`, `owner_user_id`, `column_map` |
| `bastion_decide_applicability` | Record Statement of Applicability decisions. framework_id + decisions: decide several requirements by reference code at once, every control link of each code (POST /frameworks/:id/applicability). requirement_link_id + decision: decide one control-requirement link (PATCH /control-requirements/:id). "not_applicable" needs a plain reason. The decision is audited with who made it. Needs bastion.control.write. | `framework_id`, `decisions`, `requirement_link_id`, `decision` |
| `bastion_dismiss_gap` | Dismiss a gap with a reason (POST /gaps/:id/dismiss), for example when it does not apply to how the company works. Recorded in the Bastion audit log with who dismissed it; a dismissed gap stays dismissed when gaps are re-ranked. Needs bastion.gap.dismiss (owners and admins). | `gap_id`, `reason` |
| `bastion_draft_answer` | Draft one answer now, nothing saved (POST /answers/draft): the drafted answer and yes/no, the confidence band ("Sure", "Check this", "Needs an expert") with the reasons, and the citations that passed can_access for the caller. Same hard rules as the autofill. Needs bastion.questionnaire.write. | `question`, `answer_kind`, `asker_user_id` |
| `bastion_evidence_for` | The evidence recorded for one control, newest first (GET /controls/:id/evidence): each snapshot's outcome (pass, fail, unprovable with a reason), the check that produced it, its window, counts, its hash and ledger position, and a plain explanation. Names and cited records are shown only to owners, admins and the control's owner; everyone else gets counts. Optional assertion_id narrows to one check. Pass next_cursor back as cursor while has_more is true. Read-only. | `control_id`, `assertion_id`, `cursor`, `limit` |
| `bastion_get_evidence` | One evidence snapshot by id (GET /evidence/:id), with the same counts-only rule as bastion_evidence_for. Read-only. | none |
| `bastion_get_policy_body` | The exact text of a policy's current version as Bastion serves it for acknowledgement (GET /policies/:id/body): title, Beacon version, the frozen body in Markdown, its SHA-256, Beacon's expiry, and whether the caller is asked to read it and is due. Refused with POLICY_NOT_SHARED when the article is no longer shared with everyone, and POLICY_NOT_READABLE when the caller cannot open it in Beacon. Reading it never acknowledges it: only the person can, signed in to Bastion. Read-only; needs bastion.attestation.read. | none |
| `bastion_get_questionnaire` | One questionnaire (GET /questionnaires/:id) and, with include_items (default true), its answer grid (GET /questionnaires/:id/items): each question, the drafted answer and yes/no, the confidence band and why, the citations (controls and policies, re-checked with can_access for the caller and, with asker_user_id, for that person too), the review state, and what still blocks approval ("N Needs an expert answers still to review"). | `bastion_get_settings` | Bastion's settings for the organization (GET /settings): remediation mode, check time and timezone, AI provider and the providers available, expiry warnings, staleness, auditor-link and packet limits, questionnaire floors and limits; plus evidence integrity (seal and personal-data key fingerprints, restores awaiting an owner's acknowledgement), the off-site evidence backup status and how many deliveries failed. | `bastion_get_setup_status` | Where Bastion setup stands (GET /setup): adopted frameworks and their latest compile run, the company profile, the Compliance project, the proposed checks waiting for a person (with any lists of systems awaiting "This is the complete list"), ISO applicability progress, and which required steps are done. Read-only; the setup steps themselves are done by a person in Bastion. | none |
| `bastion_link_manual_evidence` | Link uploaded evidence to a manual check it proves (POST /manual-evidence/:id/links).
